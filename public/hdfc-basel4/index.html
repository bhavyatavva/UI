Security & Confidentiality Policy

Zero Credential Exposure

All stakeholder credentials are confidential and are never pre-filled or displayed on screen. Each stakeholder must authenticate individually using their assigned HDFC Bank or RBI credentials.

Strict Segregation of Duties
  • Makers cannot approve their own regulatory returns.
  • IT Admins govern RBAC & users without self-approving filings.
  • RBI Supervisors access only certified Approved / Published reports.
  • No role-switching dropdowns inside the workspace.
